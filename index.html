Understanding Larsonism

Larsonism (Acute Goofiness & Situational Bewilderment Syndrome) is a widespread, non-contagious quirk characterized by sudden lapses in common sense, accidental physical comedy, and full confidence in terrible ideas.

Common Symptoms

  • Pulling aggressively on doors clearly labeled "PUSH".
  • Staring into the fridge for 4 minutes and closing it empty-handed.
  • Saying "You too" when a waiter says "Enjoy your food."
  • Looking for your phone while holding it in your hand.
  • Laughing at your own joke before finishing the setup.

Stages of Progression

Stage 1 (Mild): Losing sunglasses while wearing them on top of your head.

Stage 2 (Moderate): Trying to unlock your front door with your car key fob.

Stage 3 (Peak Larsonism): Zero situational awareness paired with maximum goofy confidence.

Treatment & Care

There is no known cure. Doctors recommend deep breaths, snack breaks, and embracing the bit.
