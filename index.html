Understanding Larsonism

Larsonism (Acute Goofiness & Situational Bewilderment Syndrome) is a widespread, non-contagious neurological quirk characterized by sudden lapses in common sense, inexplicable physical comedy, and confident execution of terrible ideas.

Common Symptoms

  • Aggressively pulling on doors clearly labeled "PUSH".
  • Staring blankly into the refrigerator for 4+ minutes without taking anything.
  • Saying "You too" when a waiter tells you to enjoy your meal.
  • Walking into a room and immediately forgetting why you entered.
  • Laughing at your own joke before finishing the punchline.

Stages of Progression

Stage 1 (Mild): Occasional mispronunciation of simple words and losing sunglasses while wearing them on your head.

Stage 2 (Moderate): Attempting to unlock your front door using your car key fob.

Stage 3 (Peak Larsonism): Full commitment to goofy behavior in public with zero situational awareness.

Treatment & Management

There is currently no known cure for Larsonism. Doctors recommend hydration, double-checking door signs, and accepting your fate.

If you or a loved one exhibit two or more symptoms daily, please obtain an official certificate for workplace or school accommodations.
